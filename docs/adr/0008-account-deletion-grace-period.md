# Stop work immediately and delete personal data after 30 days

When an OpenBot user account is deleted, the system immediately disables that user's private OpenMuse Bot and outstanding tasks and disconnects their Google connection. The user's private files and business records remain recoverable by an administrator for 30 days, then are deleted from OpenBot's stores. A durable deletion record makes cleanup retries safe.

Immediate data deletion would make mistaken account removal irreversible. Indefinite retention would leave personal mail-derived files and task history without a bounded cleanup date. The grace period separates immediate loss of access and credentials from delayed removal of recoverable data.
